# Code of conduct

Wanaflow is built by people with different backgrounds, languages, and levels
of experience. We want participation to feel thoughtful, direct, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing the work itself.
- Give concrete, actionable feedback and make room for questions.
- Welcome contributors who are new to BPMN, DMN, workflow engines, or the codebase.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and responsible security disclosure.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully when scope or safety requires a boundary.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized
conduct, doxxing, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in project
spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
GitHub contact options. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation depending
on severity and history. Reports will be handled with discretion and without
retaliation.
